Constitutional Foundation

How the Augmented Bill of Rights connects to the U.S. Constitution

Fourth Amendment

Unreasonable searches and seizures

Protects neural data from warrantless government access. Establishes "neural expectation of privacy" test.

Articles: III, XIII, XXVIII
Ninth Amendment

Unenumerated rights retained by the people

Foundation for cognitive liberty, bodily autonomy, psych-continuity, and self-determination rights.

Articles: II, V, VI, VIII, IX, XX, XXXV, XXXVI
Fourteenth Amendment

Equal Protection and Due Process

Prohibits discrimination based on augmentation status. Requires due process before deprivation of augmentations.

Articles: II, IV, V, VII, XIX, XXII, XXIII, XXIV, XXV, XXVII
First Amendment

Freedom of thought and expression

Protects cognitive liberty, cultural expression through augmentations, and freedom from coercive influence.

Articles: IX, XII, XIV, XXII, XXXII
Fifth Amendment

Due process and property rights

Protects against compelled neural data disclosure. Establishes data ownership as property right.

Articles: III, VIII, XVII, XIX, XXIX
Commerce Clause

Federal regulation of interstate commerce

Enables federal enforcement of augmented rights across state lines and in smart city infrastructure.

Articles: VII, XI, XVIII, XXI, XXIV, XXVII, XXXIII, XXXIV

Accessible Grievance Process

Article XV: Every citizen has the right to an accessible grievance process

Step 1: File Complaint

Submit complaint through accessible interface compatible with assistive technology

• Neural interface compatible
• Voice input supported
• Text-based option available
• Privacy-preserving submission

Step 2: Initial Review

OCAR reviews complaint within 30 days

• Automated policy evaluation
• Rights violation detection
• Preliminary findings report
• Interim remedies if needed

Step 3: Investigation

Full investigation with evidence collection

• Privacy-preserving audit
• Witness statements (optional)
• Policy documentation review
• Expert consultation

Step 4: Decision

Written decision with explanation

• Findings of fact
• Legal analysis
• Remedies ordered
• Accessible format provided

Step 5: Remedies

Effective remedies implemented

• Injunctive relief
• Compensatory damages
• Policy changes
• Systemic improvements

Step 6: Appeal

Right to independent review

• Administrative appeal
• Judicial review available
• No exhaustion required
• Private right of action
