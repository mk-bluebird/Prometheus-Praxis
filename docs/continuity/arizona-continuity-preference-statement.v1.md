# Arizona Continuity Preference Statement

## Purpose and scope

I provide this statement to communicate my personal preferences concerning my existing assistive, connected, implanted, and other functionally significant systems.

This document is intended as a communication and continuity aid. It is not, by itself:

- A Health Care Power of Attorney
- A Living Will
- A prehospital medical care directive
- A clinical order
- A diagnosis
- A medical-device safety determination
- A declaration that a system is life-sustaining
- A waiver of emergency-care duties
- A request for treatment, removal, installation, reconfiguration, or programming of any device
- A substitute for professional clinical judgment, applicable law, or a valid Arizona advance directive

This statement should be read together with any valid Arizona advance directive, healthcare-agent designation, device documentation, and relevant clinical records.

## Personal preference

I request that existing systems that support my communication, autonomy, privacy, mobility, daily functioning, safety, or wellbeing be treated as functionally significant to me.

For any non-urgent proposed removal, disabling, reconfiguration, separation from required accessories, access restriction, or material functional limitation, I request:

1. Accessible explanation of the proposed action and reason.
2. An individualized functional-impact assessment.
3. Consideration of less-disruptive feasible alternatives.
4. Meaningful opportunity to ask questions and express preferences.
5. Involvement of my chosen support person, advocate, or healthcare agent where appropriate.
6. Consultation with appropriate manufacturer, technical, clinical, or accessibility support where feasible.
7. Written documentation of the decision, scope, responsible party, expected duration, and reassessment plan.
8. A repair, replacement, restoration, or equally effective accommodation plan when function is interrupted.

## Emergency circumstances

If a genuine emergency requires immediate action, I request that responders and clinicians use the least disruptive feasible measure consistent with the circumstances and applicable professional duties.

When immediate action occurs before meaningful discussion is possible, I request that the following occur as soon as practicable:

1. The reason, observed risk, action taken, and affected system/function are documented.
2. I receive an accessible explanation.
3. My designated support person, advocate, or healthcare agent is notified when appropriate.
4. Any continuing restriction is promptly reassessed.
5. Communication access, distress signaling, and other essential functions are preserved or replaced with an equally effective accessible alternative where feasible.
